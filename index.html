About Me

關於我

Hello everyone! I'm Janice (HeJia), the founder of BojianaShanti. Welcome to a place where we explore the world of Eastern and Western aroma and create kindness together.

大家好，我是 BojianaShanti 的主理人和佳，歡迎來到一個探索東西方香學，以善意為出發點的地方。

My story

Sometimes, when a person is in the most pain, numbness becomes the norm. We want to protect ourselves, so body and mind come apart.

In 2021, I felt as if I were walking through a dark, endless tunnel under the sea. The space I had to live in kept shrinking until I could hardly breathe. I still worked hard at my job, but in private, the answer to who I was seemed to be lost.

Aromatherapy was like a key for me. I still remember that bottle of clary sage essential oil: the moment I smelled it, I felt my body and soul connect again for the first time in months. From a scientific point of view, smell acts directly on the limbic system, the brain's centre for emotion and memory. That is why scent is so powerful.

That was the start of my aromatherapy studies. In 2022 I earned an aromatherapy certification issued by Canjune in Taiwan and Usha Veda in Switzerland. As I went deeper, in 2023 I came across the Chinese incense tradition. Incense and medicine share the same root, and the Chinese system of incense is closely tied to Traditional Chinese Medicine. Born and raised in Taiwan, I grew up seeing TCM doctors and was truly helped by them many times. My grandmother studied Chinese medicine. Throughout my childhood she used what she knew to look after family and friends. I admired her and wanted to be like her, and that planted in my heart the seed of wanting to help others.

My work was always busy, so I treated learning as a personal interest. But then I figured out that I was spending at least 15 to 20 hours a week on classes, and when I looked back, apart from trips back to Taiwan, every time I went abroad it was to study aromatherapy. In 2025, I flew to Provence to study aromatherapy and to visit the factories, to see in person how essential oils and hydrosols were made. In the same year, I also flew to Beijing eight times to study! My job and my responsibilities required me to stay on call, so many times I worked between classes and late at night to keep up the quality of my work. I don't know how I survived all the red-eye flights. It seems a little crazy, but I want to thank myself for trying so hard.

Finally, in 2026, I made up my mind to leave the job I had been in for almost ten years and spent five months in Beijing studying full time. I am still learning, but I think I am ready to share now.

背景故事

有時候一個人在最痛的時候，麻木反而是一個常態，因為我們想要好好地保護自己，於是身體和意識就分開了。

在 2021 年的時候，我就好像在永無止盡、黑暗的海底隧道裡往前走。我感覺我的生存空間被擠得越來越小，快要不能呼吸。工作的時候我還是很努力，但私底下關於「我是誰」卻好像越來越模糊。

芳療對我而言，就好像是一把鑰匙。還記得那是一瓶快樂鼠尾草精油，我一聞到它，馬上感覺到我的身體和靈魂重新「黏」在了一起。當然，以科學的角度來解釋，嗅覺可以直接影響腦部的邊緣系統，也就是情緒和記憶中心。這也是氣味帶來的影響比我們想像中更大的原因。

從此我開啟了學習芳療之旅，在 2022 年拿到了由台灣肯園跟瑞士 Usha Veda 頒發的芳療證照。隨著學習的深入，我在 2023 年接觸到了中式香學，而且香藥同源，中國的香學體系跟中醫有很大的關係。身為一個土生土長在台灣長大的人，我從小就很習慣去看中醫，而且真的曾經受到過很多幫助。我外婆學過中醫，從小到大她用中醫相關知識照顧身邊的親朋好友，讓我很崇拜和嚮往，也在我心中埋下了想要幫助他人的種子。

我的工作一直很忙碌，所以關於學習，我一直把它當成個人的興趣。但仔細想想，除了回台灣以外，我每次出國都是為了學習芳療而去。2025 年我飛去了薰衣草的故鄉普羅旺斯學習芳療和拜訪工廠，同一年我還飛了北京 8 次去學習，為了節省時間都是坐紅眼航班。另外因為我的工作需要時常回覆訊息和做報告，有好多次我是在課間和熬夜趕報告，來維持工作的品質。現在回想，都覺得自己好瘋狂呀，也想謝謝這麼努力的我自己。

最後，在 2026 年我下定決心告別待了快十年的工作，去北京專心學習了五個月。我還在學習的路上，但我想我現在準備好來分享了。

Where the name comes from

Bojiana is Jia placed inside Bona: Bo + jia + na. Shanti completes the name.

  • Bona From bona fide. It stands for the kindness I received at my lowest point. I have always been grateful for it, and I want to pass that kindness on whenever I can.
  • Jia From HeJia (和佳), the incense name my Shifu (teacher) gave me. I love this name because in Chinese it sounds like 何家, which can be understood as “where is home?” I take it to mean: wherever my heart is at peace, that is home. In the hustle and bustle of this world, I hope this place brings you the warmth of home.
  • Shanti A Sanskrit word meaning peace, and a wish that everyone may reach complete peace in body, mind and spirit.

名字的由來

Bojiana 是把 Jia 放進 Bona 的中間：Bo + jia + na，最後再加上 Shanti。

  • Bona 來自 bona fide，代表我在最低谷的時候曾經接收到的善意。我一直很感激，也想要在有機會的時候把善意傳播出去。
  • Jia 來自我師父給我的香名「和佳」。我很喜歡這個名字，因為它在中文有個諧音「何家」，可以解釋為「何處為家」，我把它引申為「我心安處即是我家」。在喧囂的世界裡，希望這個地方能帶給你家的溫暖。
  • Shanti 源於梵語，意思是「平靜」，祝福大家都能在身心靈中達到全然的平靜。

The whale shark

The whale shark in the logo carries a message: strength can be gentle, and gentleness is a strength of its own.

I used to feel very small, like a snail without its shell. Softness seemed to be no help in protecting me from all the unexpected storms. I tried to be tough, but that was just not my way of dealing with trouble.

What really helped was finding that even in the darkest night, I still had the power to choose. Even the tiniest candle can light up the sky. That is where I found my resilience.

And I am not small. After coming out of the undersea tunnel, I figured out that I am the whale shark. I am happy that I can explore this beautiful world again.

I sincerely hope the world treats us gently. All the dark nights are just a reminder of how strong we are.

Let us pass kindness on, and we can light up the whole world with love.

鯨鯊

Logo 中的鯨鯊代表的是溫柔的強大：強大也可以很溫柔，溫柔也是一種強大。

我曾經覺得自己好渺小，只能被迫接受，好像一直被傷害而且沒有選擇的權利。但走過以後發現，原來我自己就可以是鯨鯊，在海洋裡遨遊，重新發現這個世界的美好。

我真誠盼望大家都能被世界溫柔以待。請記得，我們都是有力量的。

讓我們把善傳出去，每個人都能成為提燈人，照亮整個世界。

What you can expect

    Which of the herbal teas you see everywhere in Singapore suits you? When winter comes, is sesame oil chicken the right choice for you? I will introduce all kinds of fun and practical ways of aromatic care, because health matters most.

    When the outside world is so noisy, it is important for us to go back to the classics. Everything changes so fast, but the history we know does not. There are so many hidden gems there. I want to introduce what is still so beneficial today, and all the fun facts from history!
